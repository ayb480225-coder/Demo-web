Packages

Multi-session treatment plans

Packages combine repeated therapy sessions with a doctor review. Book the first slot online; the clinic schedules the remaining sessions with you.

How packages work

  1. Book the first 30-minute slot and pay at the clinic.
  2. The doctor confirms the therapy sequence and any diet changes.
  3. Reception books your remaining sessions around your schedule.
  4. Missed sessions are rescheduled by calling the clinic.
